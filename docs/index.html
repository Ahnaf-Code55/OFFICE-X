Keep your office. Add your teammates.

Each person keeps their own agents and chooses what teammates may do on their device. Shared presence lets everyone see who is in the room without merging local agent or terminal state.

01 / TASK REQUESTS

You approve each task

A teammate can send a request into your approval history. You decide whether to start work with your local agents.

02 / DELEGATED

Steer selected agents

Allow a pinned teammate to send prompts to eligible agents running on your computer.

03 / FULL CONTROL

Share terminal control

Allow terminal input. Terminal output remains off until you enable it for that teammate and agent.

Choose how your offices connect.

Both teammates configure a reachable route. The relay carries encrypted peer traffic; it does not invite teammates or grant permissions.

01 / MANAGED

OQVIAN relay

Use the managed Circuit Relay v2 service when an OQVIAN deployment has configured its endpoint.

02 / DIRECT

Peer to peer

Connect directly using a reachable public TCP route. Router and firewall forwarding may be needed.

03 / SELF-HOSTED

Your relay

Run the included Circuit Relay v2 host and configure its multiaddress on both devices.

Deployment status: this repository does not include a live OQVIAN managed relay endpoint. Managed mode must be configured by the deployment owner.
